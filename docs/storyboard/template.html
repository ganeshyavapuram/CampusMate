Meet Priya

Priya is a BCA 6th-semester student at IGNOU. She studies after her part-time job, on her phone and a shared family laptop. Her timetable is in a calendar app, her notes are PDFs on Google Drive, deadlines arrive in a WhatsApp group, and she counts attendance on her fingers. Every story below is a moment in her week.

1. Read the momentWhat Priya does, in one or two sentences.
2. Check the analogyAn everyday comparison that shows what the feature or decision is for.
3. Read whyWhat goes wrong without it, or what you give up by approving.
4. DecideApprove, ask for changes, or reject, and add a note. Where a story has an open question, accept our recommendation or leave a note with your own answer.
5. SyncAsk Claude to sync the storyboard. Your decisions and accepted recommendations are written into the story and decision files in the repository, and the "In repo" label updates.

Part two

Decisions: how it will be built

Each decision (an ADR, Architecture Decision Record) is a technical choice and what it costs. "Accepted" means the team has already made this choice; "Proposed" means it's new or changes the synopsis and should be agreed with your Project Guide. Approving a decision marks it accepted; rejecting it marks it rejected.
